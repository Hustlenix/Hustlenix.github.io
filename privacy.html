Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

What we collect

Hustlenix runs Infinite Colour Craft as a free browser game with no accounts and no sign-ups. We do not ask for your name, email, or any personal details. Your game progress (unlocked colors, board, palettes) is stored only in your own browser's local storage on your device — it is never sent to us.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which helps keep the game free. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, including for ad personalization and measurement. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com. Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent message to accept or manage these choices.

Third parties

Embedded content (such as ads) may set its own cookies subject to the third party's own policies. We do not control those cookies.

Children

The game is a general-audience coloring game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions? Contact us via the contact page.
