About PCBOD

Printed Circuit Boards On Demand.

PCBOD is a startup concept for a creator marketplace where electronics designers publish production-ready PCB products, buyers order physical versions, manufacturing is coordinated through partners, and creators earn royalties without carrying inventory.

POSITIONING

Publish hardware like software.

IMMEDIATE FOCUS

PCB-based electronics: bare boards, assembled PCBs and kits.

OPERATING MODEL

Partner manufacturing and admin-managed fulfillment first; deeper automation later.

Current status

PCBOD is currently being developed as a startup concept and prototype. This public website explains the intended marketplace and fulfillment model. It does not currently process transactions, accept real production files, operate a factory, or represent concept listings as live inventory.
