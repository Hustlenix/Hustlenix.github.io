01 / ABOUT ME

I am interested in one question more than anything else:what helps people understand themselves and each other better?

That question has taken me through counselling psychology, emotional intelligence, transactional analysis, learning and development, instructional design, education, training, public speaking and leadership.

I do not see those as separate careers. They are different ways of doing the same work: helping people notice what is happening, make sense of it, and move forward with more clarity.

MY WORK IN ONE LINE

Make human behaviour easier to understand — and useful in everyday life.
