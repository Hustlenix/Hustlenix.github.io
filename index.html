What is this?

Infinite Colour Craft by Hustlenix is a color alchemy game and paint studio. You combine pigments two at a time to unlock new colors, work through quests and a daily challenge with streaks, save palettes, and paint in a full studio with sound synthesized live from your brush strokes. An on-device neural network even guesses your doodles in the AI Lab.

Why colors?

Most digital color tools hand you a picker. This one makes color the game itself: every shade is earned by understanding how pigments combine, which turns an afternoon of play into a genuine intuition for hue, saturation, and lightness. Artists use it to warm up; everyone else just gets hooked discovering what Gold mixed with Cosmic makes.
