06 — RULES

Minimal idea.
Clear contract.

Is the Lenovo the laptop being purchased?

No. The Lenovo IdeaPad is the physical sponsor canvas. The campaign target is the future Framework Laptop 16.

Does a sponsor legally own part of the Lenovo?

No. The percentage describes physical advertising surface, not ownership of the laptop.

What if Framework pricing changes?

The component data in this site can be updated and the treemap automatically recomputes every zone from the new prices.

What is not included in the current target?

India procurement, taxes, import duties, forwarding, delivery and other logistics are not included in this hardware-only planning estimate.
