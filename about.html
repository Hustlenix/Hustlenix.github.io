About

Hustlenix is an independent hobby project building small, free creative tools for the browser. Our main project is Infinite Colour Craft, a game where you discover colors by mixing pigments the way physical paint behaves — blue plus yellow makes green, not grey — across 150+ named pigments, nine color families, and six rarity tiers.

The game is free, runs entirely in your browser with no account and no downloads, and includes a paint studio with live-synthesized brush sounds, daily challenges with streaks, quests, palettes, real-time collaboration, and an AI Lab where an on-device neural network guesses your doodles.

Alongside the game we publish written guides on color mixing and gameplay, so players can learn real color intuition — hue, saturation, lightness, and how artists think about pigment — while they play.
