The idea

Instead of hiding sponsors in a description, I’ll put them on the actual laptop.

The centre of the lid is the most expensive spot. Corners cost less. A company buys a spot, I use that money toward the Framework 16, and their artwork gets printed and stuck on the machine. Regular viewers can support with UPI without buying ad space.

The point is to see whether a physical object can be funded by turning the object itself into the ad inventory.

Fine print

Where does the money go?

To the Framework 16 build and directly related campaign costs. The target and running total should stay public.

What if the goal isn’t reached?

The final result should still be published, along with a clear explanation of how collected money is handled.

Who handles payments?

Payment accounts and any sponsor agreements should be handled with a parent or guardian where legally required.

Is Framework involved?

No. This is an independent experiment and is not sponsored, endorsed or run by Framework Computer Inc.
