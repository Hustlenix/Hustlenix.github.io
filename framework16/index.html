05 / TRANSPARENCY

Where does the money go?

Campaign funds are intended for the Framework Laptop 16 build and directly related project costs. The target and progress should be updated publicly.

What does a sponsor get?

A physical branded placement plus the campaign deliverables stated in that sponsor package. No guaranteed view count is promised.

What happens if the goal isn’t reached?

Publish the final result honestly and state how collected funds will be handled before accepting payments.

Who handles payments?

Because the creator is under 18, payment accounts, contracts and settlement should be controlled by a parent or guardian where required.
