Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Information Hustlenix asks you to provide

The Hustlenix website and Infinite Colour Craft do not require a user account. The site does not ask you to submit your name, phone number, postal address, or payment information to use the public pages or browser game.

Browser storage

Infinite Colour Craft uses browser storage for features such as unlocked colors, saved palettes, preferences, and progress. This data is stored on the device/browser you use. Clearing site data may remove that locally stored progress.

Doodle recognition

The doodle recognition feature runs its model in the browser. The drawing is processed locally for inference rather than being uploaded to a Hustlenix server for recognition.

Advertising

This site contains Google AdSense integration code and may display Google advertising when the site and advertising account are eligible. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and—where permitted and consented to—personalization. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's partner-sites information page.

Where consent or privacy choices are legally required, the advertising configuration must provide the applicable consent controls before personalized advertising is used.

External links

Pages may link to GitHub, Google documentation, or other external websites. Those services have their own privacy practices. Hustlenix does not control data collected after you leave this site.

Children

The browser game is designed as a general creative activity and does not require personal information to play. Hustlenix does not intentionally ask children to submit personal information through this site.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site adds or changes features, analytics, advertising, or other third-party services. The current revision date is shown at the top of this page.

Questions

For questions or corrections about this policy, use the methods on the contact page.
