06 — TRANSPARENCY

Clear rules before money moves.

Where does the money go?

To the Framework 16 build and directly related campaign costs. The public target should be updated when the configuration changes.

What happens if the target is not reached?

The result should still be published. Before accepting payments, the campaign should state exactly how collected funds will be handled if the build cannot proceed.

What does a sponsor actually buy?

A defined physical placement plus the campaign deliverables stated in that package. Sponsorship is advertising, not a charitable donation.

Who controls payments?

Where legally required, payment accounts and sponsor agreements should be handled or approved by a parent or guardian.
